> **Purpose:** Production-ready video prompts for the site's cinematic surfaces.
> **Style system:** Photorealistic · 8K · Cinematic · luxury lighting · editorial brand film.

## Usage notes

- **Delivery:** MP4 (H.264) + WebM fallback; poster frames from the matching image prompts.
- **Looping:** all loops are designed with matched start/end frames; keep playback muted + autoplay, add a play/off control for accessibility.
- **Performance:** lazy-load video only when section is near viewport; use `preload="none"` until needed.
- **Reduced motion:** when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set, serve a static poster instead of autoplay video.
