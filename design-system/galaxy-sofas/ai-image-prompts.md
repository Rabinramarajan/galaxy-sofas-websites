## Usage notes

- **Aspect ratios:** hero/banners 16:9 (desktop) + 4:5 (mobile); cards & categories 3:4 or 4:5; materials/craft 4:3; gallery mixed masonry; product close-ups 1:1.
- **Consistency:** keep the warm ivory/espresso/gold palette and golden-hour lighting across a set; reuse the same sofa or room from prompts 01, 08, 61 for hero consistency.
- **Export:** generate at 2x target display size, deliver as WebP (hero: AVIF where supported), strip metadata.
- **Lazy loading:** hero image `fetchpriority="high"`, all others `loading="lazy"` per the codebase convention.
